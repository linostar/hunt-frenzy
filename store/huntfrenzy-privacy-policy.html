Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy covers Hunt Frenzy, a single-player arcade game by linostar for Android and the web.

The short version

Hunt Frenzy does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What the game stores, and where

To keep your progress, the game saves a small file on your own device (on Android, in the app's private storage). If that file is ever damaged, the game keeps a backup copy of it there too. It holds only game data:

  • your campaign progress: the stars and best score of each level you have played;
  • your best scores in Endless and Time Attack;
  • your settings: music and sound volumes, their mute switches, and Reduce motion.

This file never leaves your device. The game cannot read it from anywhere else, and nobody else can see it. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes it. In the web version, your browser keeps the same data in its own storage for the game's site, and clearing that site's data in your browser deletes it.

Permissions

The Android app requests no permissions: no internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access.

Children

Hunt Frenzy collects no personal information from anyone, so it collects none from children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party services. The store or website you get it from (such as Google Play or itch.io) has its own privacy policy for your account and downloads there.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/linostar/hunt-frenzy/issues.
