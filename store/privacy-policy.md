# Hunt Frenzy: privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Hunt Frenzy is a game by linostar. This policy applies to the Android app
(`com.uniquity.huntfrenzy`) and to the browser version on itch.io.

## Data that the game collects

None. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no online features.
- The game shows no advertisements and contains no analytics or tracking code.
- The Android app asks for no permissions. It does not use the internet, the camera,
  the microphone, your location or your contacts.

## Data that stays on your device

The game saves your progress, best scores and settings (volume, mute, reduce motion) in
a file on your device (Android) or in your browser's storage (itch.io). This file never
leaves your device. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear the app's storage
(Android), or clear the site data in your browser.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect data from anyone, children included.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/linostar/hunt-frenzy/issues.
